How Much Does a Divorce Cost?

A divorce in the US costs $500 to $50,000 or more.

Divorce Cost Guide is an informational guide, not a law firm and not legal advice. This page gives general cost information only.

For full details, see the guide to divorce cost.

Costs at a glance

  • An uncontested divorce runs $500 to $4,100.
  • A contested divorce runs $15,000 to $50,000 plus.
  • The national average is about $11,300 with a lawyer, and the median is $7,000.
  • A DIY divorce costs $300 to $1,500.
  • An online divorce service costs $150 to $1,000 plus filing fees.
  • Mediation costs $3,000 to $8,000.
  • With a lawyer, an uncontested divorce costs $4,100 to $12,000.
  • Court filing fees run $70 to $435 depending on the state.
  • Divorce lawyers charge $250 to $450 an hour.

What decides the cost

The deciding factor is conflict, not state or income.
